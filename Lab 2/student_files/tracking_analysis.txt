The eigenvalue/residual categories identify different diagnostics, not uniquely proven physical causes. **λ1 large, λ2 small** means a predominantly one-dimensional gradient, as along an edge: motion along the edge is ambiguous. **Both small** means weak texture, possibly reduced further by blur. A point with **both eigenvalues above the minimum but a large residual** has enough local directional information yet violates the fitted translational/brightness-constancy model; blur, changing appearance, occlusion or a wrong/non-converged match can cause this. Invalid points with neither gate exceeded are reported separately rather than being called brightness failures. Failed propagation records are discarded when the tracker re-localises, so saved flow panels describe the retained records and do not exhaust every lost frame. [AI-LANGUAGE]

In the fast-rotation interval visible at **frames 64–96**, frames **62–63** are OK and the first following warning, at **64**, is **REINIT/brightness_change** (**0.159 > 0.15**). Its inlier ratio is still **0.708**, mean flow **12.22 px** and corner displacement **8.66 px**, all within their monitor bounds. Thus model loss takes precedence at the crossing; the saved signal cannot isolate the card as its cause from the preceding camera motion. [AI-LANGUAGE] [AI-VISION]

At the second crossing, frames **304–307** stay OK while the propagated inlier ratio falls **0.981 → 1.000 → 0.854 → 0.756**. It never crosses the 0.40 monitor threshold.
